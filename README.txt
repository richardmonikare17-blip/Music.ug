BEATBRIDGE MUSIC PROMOTION WEBSITE
==================================

FILES
- index.html: website content and sections
- style.css: layout, colours and mobile-responsive design
- app.js: demo posting feed and browser-only storage

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a web browser to preview the website.
3. Edit the files with Microsoft Expression Web or another code editor.

CUSTOMISE
- Replace BeatBridge with your chosen music-promotion brand.
- Change sample artist names and descriptions in index.html.
- Replace the example social URLs and email address with your official links.
- For a direct WhatsApp chat, edit the WhatsApp link in index.html to:
  https://wa.me/2567XXXXXXXX
  Replace the Xs with the authorised contact number in international format,
  without spaces or the plus sign.
- Change colours in the :root section at the top of style.css.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
This is a front-end demo. Posts and uploaded media are saved only in the browser
on the device where they are created. Other visitors cannot see them, and it has
no user accounts, shared database, moderation, or real public upload system yet.
To launch a live community website where every visitor sees posts and can chat,
you will need hosting plus a backend/database and secure authentication. Social
buttons currently open the main service websites; replace them with your own
artist/community profile links.

Only upload music, photos, and posters you own or have permission to share.
